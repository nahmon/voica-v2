## 1. VoicePanel (voicepanel.com)

**Positioning:** AI-native voice/video interview platform — conducts interviews autonomously at scale.

| Plan | Monthly Price | Annual Price | Included | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | Limited | Basic access, limited responses |
| Pro | ~$99/mo | Unclear (14-day trial offered) | Unlimited completes (own users) | Voice/video/phone AI interviews, AI thematic analysis, AI exec summaries, chat with data, 29 languages, Slack integration, unlimited viewers/editors |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom | Whitelabel, custom domain, API access, SOC 2 Type II, custom integrations, priority support |

**Recruiting credits:** Optional add-on for built-in B2C/B2B panel. Charged per recruited response on top of subscription.

**Target audience:** Product teams, UX researchers who want to run moderated-style interviews at scale without scheduling overhead.

## 2. Respondent.io (respondent.io)

**Positioning:** High-quality participant recruiting marketplace — focuses on B2B professional audiences.

| Plan | Price | Sessions Included | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pay-As-You-Go (B2C) | $39–40/session | Per session | Access to 3M+ participants, flexible scheduling |
| Pay-As-You-Go (B2B) | $65–80/session | Per session | Business professional targeting, screening |
| Basic Annual (B2C) | $1,980/yr (~$165/mo) | 60 sessions/yr | ~$33/session effective rate |
| Basic Annual (B2B) | $3,300/yr (~$275/mo) | 60 sessions/yr | ~$55/session effective rate |
| Advanced Annual (B2C) | $3,600/yr (~$300/mo) | 150 sessions/yr | ~$24/session effective rate |
| Advanced Annual (B2B) | $6,000/yr (~$500/mo) | 150 sessions/yr | ~$40/session effective rate |
| Custom | Contact sales | Custom | Volume discounts, custom terms |

**Note:** Pricing shown is recruiting fee only; participant incentives are billed separately on top.

**Target audience:** UX researchers and market researchers who need pre-screened, hard-to-reach B2B professional participants.

## 3. User Interviews (userinterviews.com)

**Positioning:** End-to-end research recruiting platform with both a marketplace (Recruit) and panel management (Research Hub).

### Recruit (participant sourcing from their panel)

| Plan | Monthly Price | Annual Price | Sessions Included | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pay-As-You-Go | $49/session | — | Per session | No commitment, 1 project at a time |
| Essential | $300/mo | $250/mo | 15 sessions/mo (rollover) | Unlimited projects, scheduling, screeners, incentives |
| Premium | Contact sales | Contact sales | Custom | Dedicated support, advanced features |
| Enterprise | Contact sales | Contact sales | 1M+ contacts | Custom SLA, SSO, advanced security |

### Research Hub (own panel / CRM)

| Plan | Monthly Price | Key Features |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Basic panel management, limited features |
| Workflow | ~$250/mo | Automated invites, screening, scheduling, incentive payments |
| CRM | Higher tier | Full CRM, CSV/integration imports, segmentation |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited seats, dedicated support |

**Target audience:** Research ops teams running ongoing studies; teams wanting to recruit from their own customer base.

## 4. Ethnio (ethn.io)

**Positioning:** Live website intercept recruiting — captures visitors actively using your product for immediate UX research sessions.

| Plan | Monthly Price | Annual Price | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Little | $79/mo | ~$66/mo | 2,000 responses/mo, 5 team members, 25K pageviews/mo, web intercept or link |
| Big | $179/mo | ~$149/mo | Higher response/pageview limits, more team members |
| Pro | $349/mo | ~$289/mo | Unlimited screeners, large scale pageviews, full feature set |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Pool access, app intercepts (iOS/Android), custom themes, white-label, multiple admins |

**Note:** Pricing scales with website pageview volume, not seat count. Sites under 10K pageviews may qualify for a free tier.

**Target audience:** Enterprise UX/design teams that need to recruit real users mid-session on live products; Netflix, Airbnb-style continuous discovery programs.

## 5. Dovetail (dovetail.com)

**Positioning:** AI-powered research repository and analysis platform — centralizes all qualitative data (notes, transcripts, recordings) with AI synthesis.

| Plan | Monthly Price | Annual Price | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 1 project, unlimited transcription & summarization, AI chat, 1 channel, unlimited free viewers |
| Professional | ~$20–29/user/mo (monthly) | $15/user/mo | Unlimited projects & channels, AI summaries, semantic search, advanced analysis, unlimited transcription & storage |
| Channels add-on | ~$60/mo (monthly) | $50/mo (500 data points) | Automated data ingestion (surveys, support tickets, etc.) |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | SSO, advanced security, compliance, dedicated support, unlimited everything |

**Note:** Professional pricing was reduced to $15/user/month (annual) in 2025. Monthly billing carries a ~20–30% premium.

**Target audience:** Research ops, product teams, and CX teams building a centralized insights repository; great for teams with lots of existing interview/recording data to analyze.

## 6. HeyMarvin / Marvin (heymarvin.com)

**Positioning:** AI-native research repository with AI moderated interviews — combines data storage with AI-powered synthesis and live interview capability.

| Plan | Monthly Price (Annual) | Min Seats | Included | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | — | 5 files/mo, 2 contributors, 40-min recordings | Basic repository, no credit card required |
| Essentials | $50/user/mo | 5 users ($250/mo min) | 30 files/mo, 5 contributors | AI tagging, insights, search, templates |
| Standard | $100/user/mo | 5 users ($500/mo min) | Unlimited files, contributors, viewers, recordings | Full AI synthesis, unlimited call recordings |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Unlimited + advanced security | AI-powered synthesis, SSO, compliance, dedicated support |

**Note:** Annual commitment required — no month-to-month option. Minimum team size of 5 users on paid plans makes entry cost $250/mo (Essentials) or $500/mo (Standard).

**Target audience:** Mid-to-large research teams wanting a single platform for conducting AI interviews, storing all qualitative data, and synthesizing insights at scale.

## Positioning Summary

| Platform | Core Strength | Best For | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| **VoicePanel** | Autonomous AI voice interviews at scale | Product/UX teams wanting scalable qual research without scheduling | Less suited as a repository; recruiting credits add cost |
| **Respondent.io** | Hard-to-reach B2B professional recruiting | Studies requiring niche professional personas | No analysis features; incentives on top of fees |
| **User Interviews** | Largest US panel + Research Hub CRM | Research ops teams with ongoing recruiting needs | Analysis not included; costs compound at volume |
| **Ethnio** | In-product live intercept recruiting | Enterprise teams doing continuous product discovery | Expensive for low-traffic sites; no AI analysis |
| **Dovetail** | AI repository + analysis (not recruiting) | Teams with existing interview data needing insights at scale | No recruiting; per-seat pricing grows fast |
| **HeyMarvin** | AI interviews + repository in one | Teams wanting end-to-end AI research workflow | Annual commitment + $250/mo minimum makes it costly for small teams |

---

## Notes on Data Quality

- VoicePanel pricing is partially opaque — the $99/mo starting price is widely cited but plan tiers beyond Pro/Enterprise are not publicly detailed.
- Respondent.io recruiting fees are separate from participant incentives (typically $30–100+ per participant).
- Dovetail's $15/user/mo is the 2025 annual rate; older sources cite $29–49/user range.
- HeyMarvin requires annual commitment; pricing confirmed via multiple review aggregators as of late 2025.
- Ethnio pricing confirmed via GetApp, G2, and the Bolt+Peters blog.
- All prices in USD. Verify directly with each vendor before purchasing as prices may change.
